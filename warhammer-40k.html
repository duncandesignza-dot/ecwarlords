What is Warhammer 40,000?

Warhammer 40,000 is Games Workshop's science-fiction wargame. Two or more players field armies of miniatures they have built and painted themselves - Space Marines, Orks, Tyranids, Chaos and many more - and battle over objectives on a table covered in terrain.

It's the club's flagship game: you'll find it on more tables than anything else, from first learning games to fully painted tournament armies.

  • Publisher Games Workshop
  • Players 2+
  • Game time 2-3 hours
  • Style Sci-fi battles

The setting

The galaxy of the far future, where there is only war.

The Imperium

Humanity at bay

A vast, crumbling empire of a million worlds, ruled in the name of an Emperor who has sat on the Golden Throne for ten thousand years - and defended by the superhuman Space Marines.

Its enemies

War on every front

The daemons and traitors of Chaos, and alien races from the ancient Aeldari and Necrons to the Orks and the planet-eating Tyranids.

How it plays

Two armies, a table of terrain and a handful of dice.

Turns

Move, shoot, charge, fight

Players take turns. Each turn your units move, shoot and charge into combat, with dice deciding every hit, wound and save.

Missions

Win on objectives

Games last five rounds. You score points by holding objectives and completing missions - not just by wiping out the enemy.

Army size

Points

Every unit has a points cost. Smaller games are around 1000 points and a standard game around 2000.
