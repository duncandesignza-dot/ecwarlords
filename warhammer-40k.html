What is Warhammer 40,000?

Warhammer 40,000 is Games Workshop's science-fiction wargame. Two or more players field armies of painted miniatures - Space Marines, Orks, Tyranids, Chaos and many more - and battle over objectives on a tabletop covered in terrain.

It's the club's flagship game: you'll find it on more tables than anything else, from first learning games to fully painted tournament armies.

  • Publisher Games Workshop
  • Players 2+
  • Game time 2-3 hours
  • Style Sci-fi battles

At the club

Every Wednesday

Pickup games

Weekly games between members of every experience level. Bring your army, or ask and we'll lend you one and teach you the rules.

League

Battle of the Bay

Our running 40k league. Current positions and session dates are on the Standings page.

Team games

3v3 mixed armies

At the end of 2025 we ran 3v3 games with teams and armies assigned by a random spin (600 pts per list, 1800 pts a side). In January 2026 two new players captained the teams, each paired with two veterans, so everyone got through a bigger game with help.

On the road

Nationals 2026

Five Warlords took Space Wolves, Imperial Knights, World Eaters, Raven Guard and Tyranids to Nationals in Cape Town in September 2026. See the Tournaments page.
