THE ROSTER

The people
behind the paint.

Officers, veterans, and this cycle's standout hobbyist — the club runs on the people who show up, week after week.

ADEPTUS ASTARTES — IRON HERALDS CHAPTER

Marika "Ironbrow" Voss

"I lost my first six games in a row. Stuck around anyway, mostly for the paint nights. Now I can't imagine a Thursday without this table."

Marika finished her 2000-point Iron Heralds army this spring — every model freehanded with the chapter's signature ember-orange trim — and used it to take the club's spring escalation league undefeated. She now runs our monthly painter clinics for newer members.
