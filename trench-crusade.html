TRENCH CRUSADE

Faith,
mud and iron.

A grim low-fantasy skirmish game set in a horrific alternate 1914, where trench warfare collides with occult horror.

What is Trench Crusade?

Trench Crusade is a skirmish wargame set in an alternate 1914, where an endless holy war rages between the faithful and the armies of Hell across trenches and no man's land.

Each player runs a small warband of a dozen or so models, so games are quick and it's easy to start. The core rules are free to download from the official Trench Crusade website.

  • Players 2
  • Warband Around 10-15 models
  • Game time 1-2 hours
  • Style Grim skirmish

The setting

History went very wrong.

The Gates of Hell

A crusade gone wrong

Centuries ago, crusading knights broke open the Gates of Hell in the Holy Land. Hell's legions poured out, and the war has raged ever since.

1914

An endless trench war

Faithful soldiers in gas masks and holy armour face devils, heretics and horrors across no man's land.

How it plays

Small warbands, brutal fights.

Activations

One model at a time

Players take turns activating a single model, so every decision counts.

2D6

Seven or more

Most actions need 7 or more on two dice, with bonus and penalty dice to swing the odds.

Campaigns

Your warband grows

Survivors gain experience and scars between games, and you recruit and re-equip as the campaign goes on.
