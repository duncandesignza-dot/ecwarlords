TRENCH CRUSADE

Faith,
mud and iron.

A grim low-fantasy skirmish game set in a horrific alternate 1914, where trench warfare collides with occult horror.

What is Trench Crusade?

Trench Crusade is a skirmish wargame set in an alternate 1914, where an endless holy war rages between the faithful and the armies of Hell across trenches and no man's land.

Each player runs a small warband of a dozen or so models, so games are quick and it's easy to start. The core rules are free to download from the official Trench Crusade website.

  • Players 2
  • Warband Around 10-15 models
  • Game time 1-2 hours
  • Style Grim skirmish

The setting

History went very wrong.

The Gates of Hell

A crusade gone wrong

In this world, crusading knights broke open the Gates of Hell in the Holy Land centuries ago. Hell's legions poured out, and the war between Heaven and Hell has raged ever since.

1914

Eight hundred years of war

By 1914 the war has become an endless trench war. Faithful soldiers in gas masks and holy armour face devils, heretics and horrors across no man's land.

The warbands

Factions fall on either side of the war.

The faithful

Defenders of humanity

The Principality of New Antioch, the Trench Pilgrims and the Iron Sultanate.

The damned

Servants of Hell

The Heretic Legion, the Cult of the Black Grail and the Court of the Seven-Headed Serpent.

How a game plays

Players take turns activating one model at a time, so every decision counts.

Activate

Pick one model, take its actions - move, shoot, charge or fight - then hand over to your opponent.

Roll 2D6

Most actions need a total of 7 or more on two dice. Bonus dice let you roll extra and keep the best two; penalty dice keep the worst.

Injuries

Wounded models roll on an injury chart - they may shrug it off, go down or be taken out for good.

Campaigns

Your warband grows

Between games, survivors gain experience and scars, you recruit new members and buy equipment. Campaigns are where the game shines.

Free to start

Rules online

The core rules and warband lists are free online, so you can read up and plan a warband before buying a single model.

The hobby side

Trench Crusade is a conversion lover's game.

Build

Kitbash everything

The game's look - gas masks, plate armour, barbed wire and horror - is perfect for converting models from many different ranges.

Paint

Mud and blood

Muted colours, weathering, rust and mud suit the setting. It's a great game for practising effects.

Terrain

No man's land

Trenches, sandbags, craters and ruined churches make a small table look incredible.
