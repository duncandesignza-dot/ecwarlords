Painters, generals, and lore-hounds gathered under one banner. Bring an army. Bring a paintbrush. Bring your worst dice rolls — we've all been there.

Eastern Cape Warlords is a wargaming group located in South Africa, interested in all things Warhammer 40K, Age of Sigmar, Kings of War, Dungeons and Dragons, Blood Bowl, and board games.
