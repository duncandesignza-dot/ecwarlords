battles.

Warhammer's fantasy side gets real love here too - several members collect and paint Age of Sigmar armies, and games get organised whenever there's a table free.

What is Age of Sigmar?

Warhammer Age of Sigmar is Games Workshop's fantasy wargame. Armies of heroes, monsters and magic - from Stormcast Eternals to Seraphon, Orruks and the forces of Chaos - clash across the Mortal Realms.

At the club, several members paint and collect Age of Sigmar armies, and it's a great step across for anyone who already plays 40k.

  • Publisher Games Workshop
  • Players 2+
  • Game time 2-3 hours
  • Style Fantasy battles

The setting

Eight Mortal Realms and a war that never ends.

The Mortal Realms

Worlds of magic

Eight realms, each made of a different kind of magic - Fire, Metal, Life, Death, Light, Shadow, Beasts and the Heavens - each with its own lands and peoples.

The war

Sigmar against the dark

The God-King Sigmar sends his Stormcast Eternals to reclaim the realms from the forces of Chaos, the undead legions of Nagash and the Orruk hordes.

How it plays

Heroes, monsters and magic on the table.

Turns

Who goes first?

Players roll off each round to see who goes first - so sometimes one side gets two turns in a row.

Phases

Magic, movement and combat

Heroes cast spells and give commands, then units move, shoot, charge and fight. Both sides strike in combat.

Army size

Points

A standard battle is around 2000 points. Spearhead boxes give you a small, ready-to-play force for quicker games.
