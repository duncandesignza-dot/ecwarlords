BOARD GAMES

Lighter games,
same table.

Not every night is about miniatures. We keep a rotating shelf of board games for a shorter game between battles - or a whole evening of them.

Why board games?

Alongside the wargames, the club keeps a rotating shelf of board games for anyone who wants something shorter or lighter - waiting for an opponent, between battles, or for a whole evening.

No painting or army building needed: sit down, learn the rules in a few minutes and play.

  • Players 2 and up
  • Game time Quick to an evening
  • Style Casual

Popular picks

Some of the most popular board games around - quick to learn and great between battles. Ask on the night what is on the club shelf, or bring your own.

Catan

3-4 players · 60-90 min

Trade, build and settle an island. A modern classic and easy to teach.

Ticket to Ride

2-5 players · 30-60 min

Collect train cards and claim railway routes across the map.

Carcassonne

2-5 players · 30-45 min

Lay tiles to build a medieval landscape of cities, roads and fields.

Pandemic

2-4 players · 45 min

Work together to stop four diseases spreading across the world. Fully co-operative.

Azul

2-4 players · 30-45 min

Draft colourful tiles to decorate a palace wall. Simple rules, sharp choices.

Codenames

4-8 players · 15 min

A word-guessing party game played in two teams. Great for a big group.

7 Wonders

3-7 players · 30 min

Draft cards over three ages to build your civilisation and its wonder.

Splendor

2-4 players · 30 min

Collect gems and buy cards to become the richest merchant.

Wingspan

1-5 players · 40-70 min

Attract birds to your wildlife reserves in this relaxed engine builder.

King of Tokyo

2-6 players · 30 min

Giant monsters roll dice and smash each other to rule Tokyo.

Blood Rage

2-4 players · 60-90 min

Lead a Viking clan to glory before Ragnarok - a natural fit for wargamers.

Dominion

2-4 players · 30 min

The game that started deck-building: grow your kingdom one card at a time.
