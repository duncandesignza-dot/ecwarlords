WHAT WE PLAY

Games we
play.

We're not a single-system club. Whatever's on the table on a given Thursday, there's a good chance one of these is being played somewhere in the room.

Warhammer 40,000

The club's flagship game and the one you'll find on the most tables on any given night. Weekly pickup games, ongoing narrative campaigns like Crusade of Embers, and regular 3v3 mixed-army experiments designed to pair newer players with veterans.

MOST TABLE TIME

Age of Sigmar

Warhammer's fantasy side gets real love here too — several members paint and collect Age of Sigmar armies (Seraphon among them), and games get organised whenever there's a table and an opponent free.

FANTASY BATTLES

Kings of War

A faster, more streamlined mass-battle alternative some of our members turn to for bigger armies on a tighter schedule. Ask around the club if you want to get a game going.

MASS BATTLE

Dungeons & Dragons

Our DnD table runs regularly enough that it competes for club-night attention with the wargaming tables — more than one Dispatch post has noted a quieter wargames night because the dice were rolling for a campaign instead.

TABLETOP RPG

Blood Bowl

Genuinely one of the club's biggest ongoing scenes. Season 1 of our league crowned the Fossil Fuels champions, we've run a standalone sevens tournament, and Season 2 is underway using the new 3rd edition rules — with a growing roster of teams and coaches every cycle.

RUNNING LEAGUE

Board Games

Not every night is about miniatures. We keep a rotating shelf of board games on hand for anyone who wants a shorter, lighter game between battles — or an entire evening of them.

CASUAL NIGHTS

Not sure which of these to try first? Come to a Thursday meet — we'll point you toward whichever table has room and a mentor free.
