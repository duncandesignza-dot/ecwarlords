What is Dungeons & Dragons?

Dungeons & Dragons is the classic tabletop roleplaying game from Wizards of the Coast. One player, the Dungeon Master, tells the story and runs the world, while everyone else plays a hero making choices and rolling dice to see how things turn out.

There's no winning or losing - just a story you build together, usually as a campaign that runs over many sessions.

  • Publisher Wizards of the Coast
  • Players A DM + 3-6 players
  • Session 3-4 hours
  • Style Roleplaying

The setting

D&D can be set anywhere the Dungeon Master imagines.

Classic settings

The Forgotten Realms and more

The best-known world is the Forgotten Realms, home of cities like Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate, with many other official worlds to explore.

Homebrew

Your own world

Many Dungeon Masters build their own world, with its own maps, gods and history - shaped by what the players do.

How it plays

The Dungeon Master sets the scene, the players say what they do, and the dice decide what happens.

Your hero

Pick a class

Create a character - a Fighter, Wizard, Rogue, Cleric or one of many others - with their own skills and story.

The d20

One roll decides

Most things are settled by rolling a 20-sided die and adding your bonus. Roll a natural 20 and something great happens.

Adventure

Explore, talk, fight

Sessions mix exploring, talking your way out of trouble and turn-based battles. Characters level up as the campaign goes on.
