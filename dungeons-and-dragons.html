What is Dungeons & Dragons?

Dungeons & Dragons is the classic tabletop roleplaying game from Wizards of the Coast. One player, the Dungeon Master, tells the story and runs the world, while everyone else plays a hero making choices and rolling dice to see how things turn out.

There's no winning or losing - just a story you build together, usually as a campaign that runs over many sessions.

  • Publisher Wizards of the Coast
  • Players A DM + 3-6 players
  • Style Roleplaying
