How to enter

Register

Send the club your name and the army or team you're bringing on WhatsApp or Facebook.

Pay your entry

The entry fee and payment details are listed with each event. Your spot is confirmed once it's paid.

Send your list

Submit your army list or team roster by the deadline in the rules pack so it can be checked before the day.

Turn up ready

Bring your army, dice, a tape measure, a copy of your list and your rules. Arrive in time for the first round.
