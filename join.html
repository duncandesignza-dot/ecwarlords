JOIN THE CRUSADE

Pull up a chair.
We'll bring the dice.

No experience, no army, and no idea what a Codex is? That's most of us on day one. Here's exactly how to get started.

How to get started

01

Show up Wednesday

Drop by any Wednesday, 7–10 PM. No sign-up needed for your first visit.

02

Borrow an army

No models yet? Use one of our loaner armies for your first three games, free.

03

Get a mentor

We pair every new member with a veteran for rules, list-building, and paint advice.

04

Follow along

Follow the club on Facebook and Instagram for game-night photos, painting showcases and club news.
