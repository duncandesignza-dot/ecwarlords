Stats and weapon profiles are pulled from the community-maintained BSData wh40k-10e project, the same source that powers the Army Builder above. We show ability and keyword names only — for the full official wording of any rule, follow the "View full rules on Wahapedia" link on the unit's datasheet, which goes to wahapedia.ru, a well-established third-party rules reference. Those links are generated from the unit name and aren't guaranteed to land exactly right for every unit — if one 404s, search Wahapedia directly.
