CLUB TOOLS

Tools for
the table.

Free utilities built for our members and anyone else who wants them — no sign-up, no ads, works on your phone at the table.

All tools

LIVE

Dice Roller

Rolls a full attack sequence — hits, wounds, saves and damage — with support for rerolls, Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Devastating Wounds and modified crit thresholds. Shows every die so you can check the maths.

Open the roller LIVE

Damage Calculator

Pit an attacker profile against a defender and get the expected damage — averaged mathematically, not rolled. The fast way to settle "which unit should I point at that thing?"

Open the calculator LIVE

Turn Tracker

Victory points, command points, battle round and phase for both players — plus an optional chess-clock timer for tournament play. Keeps state if you reload mid-game.

Open the tracker LIVE · 34 FACTIONS

Army List Builder

Pick units, set mob sizes and watch the points tally live against your chosen limit, with Rule of Three, Warlord, enhancement and transport checks as you go. 34 factions supported.

Open the builder LIVE · 35 FACTIONS

Datasheet Lookup

Searchable reference for unit stats, weapon profiles and abilities across 1,485 datasheets, so nobody's scrolling a PDF halfway through a shooting phase. Full rule wording links out to Wahapedia.

Open the lookup LIVE

Mission & Objective Tracker

Objective control and secondary mission scoring, round by round — synced with the Turn Tracker's battle round and victory points. You set the points and name your own secondaries, so it works with any mission pack.

Open the tracker LIVE · 28 FACTIONS

Every Warhammer 40,000 Faction at a Glance

All 28 currently-playable factions in one place — Imperium, Chaos and Xenos — each with a description, allegiance and role tags, and a search bar so you can jump straight to the one you're after instead of scrolling. Handy for checking what you're up against before a game, or for finding your next army.
