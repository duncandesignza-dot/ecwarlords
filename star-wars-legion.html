STAR WARS: LEGION

A galaxy
far, far away.

Squad-scale battles with Rebels, the Empire and beyond - a newer addition to club nights for anyone after something a little less grimdark.

What is Star Wars: Legion?

Star Wars: Legion is a miniatures game from Atomic Mass Games. Each player commands squads of troopers, vehicles and famous heroes from the Star Wars films and shows, fighting over objectives.

Players take turns activating one unit at a time, so both sides stay involved the whole game.

  • Publisher Atomic Mass Games
  • Players 2
  • Game time 2-3 hours
  • Scale 35mm

The setting

Ground battles from across the Star Wars saga.

Galactic Civil War

Rebels against the Empire

Stormtroopers, AT-STs and Darth Vader face Rebel troopers, Wookiees and heroes like Luke Skywalker and Han Solo.

The Clone Wars

Republic against Separatists

Clone troopers and Jedi generals battle droid armies led by the likes of General Grievous and Count Dooku.

How a game plays

Games last six rounds. Each round both players choose a command card, then take turns activating units.

Command cards

Choose a card each round. Lower numbers win priority; higher ones let you give more orders.

Activate units

Players alternate activating one unit. Each unit gets two actions - move, aim, dodge, attack and more.

Custom dice

Attack with red, black or white dice and defend with red or white dice, with hits, crits and surges.

Battle cards

Every game is different

Each player helps choose the objective, deployment and conditions from a set of battle cards, so the battlefield changes every game.

Movement tools

Templates and range rulers

Units move with movement templates and measure range with a range ruler - no tape measures needed.

The hobby side

Iconic characters and vehicles make for great painting projects.

Paint

Screen-accurate or your own

Terrain

Set the scene

Desert outposts, forest moons and city ruins - Legion terrain is a hobby project of its own.
