PRIVACY NOTICE

Your data,
plainly put.

What this website collects, why, and what you can do about it - in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: 3 October 2026

The short version: this site has no accounts, no mailing list and takes no payments. It uses Google Analytics to count visits, and the "Send us a word" form only opens WhatsApp - nothing you type is stored on this website.

Who we are

This website belongs to the Eastern Cape Warlords, a volunteer-run tabletop wargaming club in Gqeberha, South Africa. We are the responsible party for the personal information described here. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +27 72 474 9572 or through our Facebook page.

What this website collects

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, so we know which pages are useful and what to improve. When you visit, Google Analytics uses cookies to record things like:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay;
  • your device, browser and screen size;
  • your approximate location (city or region, worked out from your IP address);
  • how you found us, for example a search engine or a Facebook link.

We only see this as overall numbers and trends. It doesn't tell us your name or contact details, and we don't use it to identify you or to target you with adverts.

When you message us

The "Send us a word" form on the Join page doesn't send or save anything on this website. It puts your message together and opens WhatsApp, and it only reaches us if you press send there. Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram are handled under those apps' own privacy policies.

We use what you tell us (your name, experience and message) only to reply and to help you get started at the club. We don't sell it or pass it on to anyone else.

Other services the site relies on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Cloudflare delivers it quickly and protects it from attacks. Both handle your IP address to do this.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts, so your browser fetches them from Google.
  • Links to Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Google, GitHub and Cloudflare are based outside South Africa, so this information may be processed in other countries. They are large providers with their own data protection safeguards.

How long it's kept

Google Analytics keeps visit data for the retention period set in our account, after which Google deletes it. Messages are kept only as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry or your time at the club.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works fine without them.
  • You can install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Many browsers and ad blockers stop Google Analytics by default.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how we use it. Message us and we'll help. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Changes to this notice

If the website starts collecting anything new, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
